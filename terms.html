Tracking service

Renewlio is an adult personal subscription tracker. You enter and review records and confirm payment or cancellation outcomes. It does not execute payments, connect to banks/cards/UPI, read SMS automatically or cancel provider subscriptions. Verify actual charges and renewal dates with your provider. Forecasts, detected patterns, estimated savings and optional suggestions are not financial advice or guarantees.

Availability and reminders

Local tracking works without an account. Optional online features require connectivity and availability as shown in the app. Device reminders cover a rolling 30-day window with up to 50 pending jobs. Permissions and operating-system restrictions can delay delivery. Reopen regularly to refresh coverage and keep independent reminders for important payments.

Your data and backups

Keep encrypted backups and their passphrases safe: there is no passphrase recovery. CSV exports are not encrypted. Removing app data may remove the only local copy. Cloud-account deletion does not delete local or exported files. Read the privacy policy for processing and retention.

Free and optional paid features

Free tracking supports 10 active, trial or paused plans. Existing records, export, cancellation, deletion and restore remain available after downgrade. If offered, verified Plus enables unlimited new tracked plans and custom reminders. If offered, Family provides up to five adult accounts including the owner and optional read-only selected sharing, not a shared bank account, live ledger synchronization or automatic store Family Library sharing.

Managed cloud backup, when enabled, allows 3 Free or 30 verified paid snapshots, up to 20 MB each. Downgrade does not erase existing snapshots but can restrict further uploads until older snapshots are deleted. Availability of paid/cloud/Family features is stated in the app; a description here is not a promise that an unconfigured feature is on sale.

Google Play purchases, if offered

Google Play checkout shows the actual localized price and renewal terms. Annual subscriptions renew unless canceled in Google Play. Turning off renewal generally preserves paid access until the verified paid expiry, subject to authoritative store state such as refund or revocation. Paid offline tracking requires reverification within 72 hours and never extends beyond paid expiry. Restore requires the purchasing Google identity. Upgrades and downgrades follow the replacement terms shown by Google Play.

Follow Google's purchase/refund process and contact support for fulfillment problems. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Deleting your account/app or a tracker record does not cancel store billing or tracked provider subscriptions.
