Request deletion without the app

Email an account deletion request

If the button does not open an email app, email devendra.tampula@gmail.com with the subject Renewlio account deletion request. Send from the email used for Google sign-in where possible. Do not send passwords, verification codes, private keys or financial statements.

We may verify ownership securely before acting. We handle verified email requests within 30 calendar days after ownership verification. Email is a manual support request, not immediate automated deletion. You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion.

Delete through the app

  1. Open Settings → Account.
  2. Use Verify with Google, then select Delete cloud account within 10 minutes.
  3. Check the account shown and confirm deletion. If cleanup is interrupted, reconnect and retry.

What is deleted

Deletion removes the server account/profile, uploaded cloud snapshots, memberships/shared rows/invitations and purchase associations linked to the account. Local ledgers and exported backups/CSV remain on your devices or chosen destinations. Delete local data in Settings and exported files separately. Ordinary resolved support emails are deleted within 12 months.

The current Supabase Free plan publishes short dashboard log-retention periods; database authentication-audit writing is disabled. Provider-hosted security records remain subject to provider processing. See the privacy policy for those details and any disclosed retention exceptions.

Billing is separate

You can request account deletion without canceling a subscription first. Account deletion does not cancel Google Play billing or tracked provider subscriptions. To cancel Renewlio billing, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Other provider subscriptions must be managed through their original purchase channel.
