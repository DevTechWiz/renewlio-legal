Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Renewlio is published under the DevTechWiz developer brand. See our terms for the legal operator. Contact us for support, privacy requests or grievances at devendra.tampula@gmail.com.

Information on your device

Renewlio stores the subscription details you enter, notes, prices, billing schedules, reviewed CSV evidence, confirmed payments, cancellation records, savings confirmations and preferences in local SQLite storage. Device sandbox and operating-system protections apply; the database is not independently encrypted by Renewlio. CSV imports and recurring-payment rules run locally. Forecasts, detections and savings are estimates, not bank-confirmed transactions.

Permissions and exports

Renewlio does not read SMS automatically, connect to bank/card/UPI accounts, execute payments or cancel provider subscriptions. Files are selected through your device's picker. Notifications are optional and private by default. Optional app lock uses device authentication; Renewlio does not receive your PIN or biometric templates. No advertising or analytics SDK is included.

Manual snapshots are encrypted on your device using a passphrase you choose. The passphrase is not sent to Supabase and cannot be recovered. CSV exports are unencrypted. Files you save or share may go to a cloud document provider offered by your device; that provider's policies apply. Delete those exported files separately when no longer needed.

Optional Google account and cloud services

Local tracking does not require an account. When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Supabase process account identity and session information. Supabase holds your account identifier, email, optional chosen display name and authentication/session records. Sign-in alone does not upload your ledger.

When cloud backup is available and you explicitly choose an upload, Supabase stores the encrypted snapshot and object metadata such as its account path, size and timestamps. The passphrase stays on your device. There is no automatic upload or live ledger synchronization. Cloud availability is shown in the app.

The project's primary Supabase region is Tokyo, Japan (ap-northeast-1). Google, Supabase and their service providers may process information in other locations under their policies. India-only data residency is not claimed. See Supabase privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases and Family, when available

If paid plans are offered, Google Play processes payment information. Renewlio's verification service holds purchase tokens, account associations, product identifiers, subscription state, expiry and verification times to deliver access and prevent misuse. Renewlio does not handle card or UPI credentials. Purchase proofs and account credentials are excluded from ledger backups.

If Family is enabled and you join, the service stores memberships, invitations and your chosen display name. Only subscription names, INR amounts and billing cycles you explicitly select for sharing are sent to the household workspace, up to 100 selected records per member. Current authorized household members can see these read-only summaries. Private notes, evidence and payment history are excluded. Previously viewed copies or screenshots cannot be recalled.

Optional AI

Remote AI is currently disabled. If introduced, the app and this notice will identify the provider, processing and retention before requests are enabled. You will be able to review a minimized summary and choose whether to send it. Declining AI leaves local rules available. No raw CSV is sent to a model by the current release configuration.

Retention and deletion

  • Local records remain until you delete them or remove app data. Delete imported evidence separately or all local ledger data in Settings.
  • Account data and any uploaded cloud snapshots remain until you delete the relevant snapshots or your cloud account. In-app deletion requires recent Google verification. Cleanup removes the server identity/profile, snapshots, memberships/shared rows and purchase associations; interrupted cleanup is shown and can be retried.
  • Email account-deletion requests are handled within 30 calendar days after ownership verification.
  • Ordinary support emails are deleted within 12 months after the issue is resolved.

The current Supabase Free plan publishes 1-day API/database log retention and 1-hour dashboard authentication-audit log retention. Writing authentication audit logs into the project database is disabled; our check on 12 October 2026 found no stored entries there. Provider-hosted security logging is separate. Managed daily database backups and point-in-time recovery are not included in the current plan. Any later backup or retention change will be reviewed and disclosed before it is introduced. These plan limits are not a promise that every provider-held security record expires on the same schedule.

Local files and exported copies remain after cloud-account deletion and require separate deletion. Deleting your app/account does not cancel Renewlio billing or tracked provider subscriptions. Manage those through the original purchase channel. If a specific legal obligation requires retaining particular records, we will explain the affected records, reason and applicable period rather than claim a blanket exception.

Requests, audience and security

Renewlio is intended for adults 18 and older. Use the account deletion page or contact the inbox above for privacy questions or grievances. We may verify account ownership; never send passwords, verification codes, full statements or private keys by email. Private authorization, encrypted backups and explicit sharing reduce risks, but compromised devices and exported plaintext cannot be guaranteed secure.

We will update this notice when practices change and provide appropriate notice or consent for new uses before transmitting additional data.
